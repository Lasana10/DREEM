# DREEM phased blueprint

## Phase 0 — Dedicated production foundation

Repository separation, Supabase project, migrations, founder allowlist, RLS and Cloudflare deployment verification.

## Phase 1 — First-school operational certification

Founder activation, school setup, staff access, learner/guardian enrolment, credentials, attendance, assessment, Verified Money Trail, reporting and complete role-based evidence.

## Phase 2 — Resilience and communication

Offline command queue, conflict policies, backup/restore proof, SMS/WhatsApp/email delivery, bilingual templates and weak-network field tests.

## Phase 3 — Learning, welfare and transport depth

Assignments/content, learner interventions, safeguarding, parent cases, teacher growth, route assignment, boarding evidence, delays and AFAT mobility adapters.

## Phase 4 — Configurable institution editions

Early Years, Schools, TVET, Higher Education and Training Centre editions with Anglophone, Francophone and bilingual country packs.

## Phase 5 — Governed intelligence and interoperability

Human-reviewed AI assistance, OneRoster/LTI/Ed-Fi/Edu-API adapters, verifiable credentials, observability, evaluation and multi-institution intelligence without exposing tenant data.

Every phase must preserve the same institutional kernel: identity and relationships, tenant isolation, workflows and approvals, policy rules, financial subledger, evidence, messaging, credentials, audit, events, offline safety and human authority.
