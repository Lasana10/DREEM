# DREEM Feature Map

## Purpose

DREEM is a school operating platform that improves daily life for every person in the school ecosystem while making the institution faster, calmer, more visible, and more performant.

The platform should not feel like a generic portal. It should feel like a premium school operating system with communication, operations, guidance, and automation built in.

## Core Promise

DREEM should make it easier for each user to do their job well:

- reduce confusion
- reduce manual follow-up
- reduce lost time
- improve visibility
- improve decision-making
- improve coordination
- improve student outcomes

## User Groups

### 1. School Leadership

Leadership needs a clear view of the whole school.

What they need:

- attendance trends
- fee collection health
- academic risk signals
- staff workload visibility
- transport and safety status
- system health
- announcements and escalation control

What DREEM should do:

- show the institution in one command center
- highlight problems early
- help leaders make faster decisions
- reduce the need for scattered reports

### 2. Bursar and Finance Team

Finance needs control, traceability, and calm.

What they need:

- fee tracking
- debtor status
- receipts
- reconciliations
- scholarships and discounts
- payment reminders
- forecasting
- audit readiness

What DREEM should do:

- make finance highly visible
- reduce manual fee chasing
- show cash position clearly
- support school-level financial discipline

### 3. Teachers

Teachers need a system that supports teaching rather than adding burden.

What they need:

- class lists
- attendance
- timetable
- lesson pacing
- learner progress
- interventions
- announcements
- resource sharing

What DREEM should do:

- reduce admin load
- show who needs support
- help teachers pace lessons better
- support reflection and planning

### 4. Students

Students need clarity, guidance, and motivation.

What they need:

- assignments
- progress
- attendance
- announcements
- reminders
- support resources
- study guidance
- communication with school

What DREEM should do:

- help them understand what to do next
- make progress visible
- reduce missed deadlines
- support learning and confidence

### 5. Parents and Guardians

Parents need simple, trustworthy, and timely information.

What they need:

- fee alerts
- attendance summaries
- academic progress
- official announcements
- transport updates
- simple explanations
- school communication

What DREEM should do:

- keep parents informed without overload
- make school communication easier
- reduce confusion and repeated calls
- improve trust between school and family

### 6. Support and Technical Staff

Technical staff need visibility into the platform and hardware.

What they need:

- server health
- sync state
- Pi status
- alerts
- storage usage
- uptime
- service failures

What DREEM should do:

- make infrastructure health visible
- reduce downtime
- help staff react quickly to problems

### 7. Transport and Bus Staff

Transport must be treated as a real operational module, not an afterthought.

What they need:

- routes
- bus lists
- student pickup lists
- trip schedules
- delay alerts
- safety status
- driver/attendant actions

What DREEM should do:

- make routing and attendance easier
- give parents transport visibility
- reduce confusion around pickup and drop-off
- support school safety and logistics

## Communication Layer

DREEM should include a structured communication system, not just chat.

### Features to include

- school announcements
- class or group discussions
- staff rooms
- department groups
- parent groups
- transport groups
- support/help channels
- threaded conversations
- pinned official messages
- searchable history

### Design principle

It should feel a little like Slack in convenience, but much more structured and school-focused.

## Knowledge and Guidance Layer

DREEM should empower people with knowledge, not just data.

### Features to include

- guides
- SOPs
- FAQs
- learning resources
- student help content
- staff onboarding
- technical support notes
- school policies
- AI-generated summaries

### Why this matters

This makes DREEM helpful even when users are not sure what to do next.

## Bus and Transport Module

The transport module should be a first-class feature.

### Core features

- route management
- bus assignment
- student pickup lists
- attendance on the bus
- driver and attendant view
- route delay alerts
- pickup and drop-off notifications
- emergency alerts
- transport analytics

### Parent value

- know when the bus is coming
- know if there is a delay
- know if a student boarded or arrived

### School value

- better safety
- better organization
- better transport accountability
- lower chaos in morning and afternoon operations

## App Direction

DREEM should exist as:

- a web app
- a mobile app
- a local Pi-facing school operating layer

### App priorities

- push notifications
- fast actions
- announcement delivery
- transport alerts
- attendance and finance quick views
- offline-friendly use

## Product Qualities

DREEM should always feel:

- premium
- calm
- fast
- clear
- trustworthy
- school-appropriate
- empowering
- operationally strong

## Differentiators

These are the traits that can make DREEM stand out strongly:

- hybrid local-plus-cloud architecture
- offline-resilient operations
- school-owned sensitive data
- role-specific experiences
- built-in communication
- transport support
- knowledge and guidance tools
- AI assistance with human control
- leadership-grade visibility

## What Still Needs To Be Built

### Product level

- full dashboard surfaces for every role
- communication and announcement system
- transport module
- support/helpdesk layer
- knowledge hub
- mobile app shell

### Technical level

- auth and permissions
- data model
- sync engine
- offline cache and queues
- Pi routing and sentinel integration
- notification delivery
- audit history

## Best Build Order

1. Build the shared app shell and navigation.
2. Add auth, roles, and permission logic.
3. Build the leadership, bursar, and teacher dashboards.
4. Add announcements and communication groups.
5. Add the transport module.
6. Add the knowledge and support layer.
7. Add offline sync and Pi-aware routing.
8. Add mobile app support and push notifications.
9. Add analytics, AI assistance, and automation.

## Working Principle

DREEM should not try to be a noisy social app.

It should be a disciplined school operating platform that helps every user do their part better while keeping the whole institution coordinated.
